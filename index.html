A Karana is half of a lunar day — lasting about 6 hours. It tells you whether the current window of time is favorable or unfavorable for starting new things.

WHAT IS YOGA?
Yoga is the combined energy of the Sun and Moon on a given day. There are 27 Yogas — some bring success and flow, others bring obstacles and delays.

WHAT IS TITHI?
Tithi is the lunar day — the Moon's phase relative to the Sun. There are 30 Tithis in a lunar month. Each carries a different energy quality for actions and decisions.
YOUR BAD TITHIS: 3rd, 8th and 13th lunar days — avoid important decisions on these days!
